## 🌐 Vision & Mission

Over **200,000 industrial manufacturing SMEs** in Germany and across Europe rely on machinery that is mechanically sound and reliable, yet completely "dark"—lacking digital monitoring, connectivity, and automated telemetry. At the same time, factory operators face a dual squeeze:
1. **Severe downtime costs & skilled labor shortages** (*Fachkräftemangel*), where a single unexpected mechanical breakdown costs between **€5,000 and €50,000**.
2. **Mandatory ESG supply chain reporting (CSRD / LkSG)**, where European OEMs (such as BMW, Volkswagen, and Bosch) require verified **Catena-X Product Carbon Footprint (PCF)** data from every supplier.

**Illuutek** bridges this critical gap. We build self-installing, hardware-enabled SaaS solutions designed from the ground up for the *Mittelstand*—delivering edge-computed predictive maintenance and automated carbon compliance without expensive enterprise consultancy or factory rewiring.

## 🚀 Our Flagship Solutions

### 🛡️ [MachineGuard](https://github.com/illuutek) — Predictive Maintenance in 10 Minutes
> *"Clip it on. Scan the QR code. Stop unplanned downtime before it happens."*

MachineGuard is a hardware-enabled IoT and AI platform that protects critical machinery (motors, pumps, gearboxes, CNC spindles, compressors) from catastrophic failure.

- **Non-Invasive Hardware**: Magnetic, battery-powered (3–5 year life, AA lithium) multi-sensor clip. Mounts in under 10 seconds without drilling or tools.
- **Onboard Edge Intelligence**: Onboard ARM Cortex-M4 MCU computes high-speed Fast Fourier Transforms (FFT), calculating RMS, Peak, and Crest Factor metrics locally on the sensor.
- **Cellular Independence (NB-IoT)**: Bypasses factory Wi-Fi and complex internal IT approvals entirely. Operates out-of-the-box in any plant environment.
- **Autonomous Anomaly Detection**: Establishes a 7-day baseline of normal machine vibration and temperature signatures; triggers multi-tier alerts (*Watch → Warning → Critical*).
- **Instant Mechanic Notifications**: Alerts sent straight to the maintenance technician via WhatsApp Business API and a German-language, mobile-first traffic-light dashboard (*Grün/Gelb/Rot*).

### ⚡ [EnergiePuls](https://github.com/illuutek) — Energy Disaggregation & Automated Catena-X ESG
> *"Turn factory power into audit-ready Scope 1 & 2 carbon accounting with a single 2-hour installation."*

EnergiePuls resolves industrial energy waste and mandatory supply chain compliance in one seamless workflow.

- **Single-Point Installation**: Three split-core Current Transformer (CT) sensors clip onto the main three-phase incoming supply at the central distribution panel. **No rewiring and zero production downtime**.
- **Edge NILM AI Engine**: High-frequency sampling (3,200 samples/sec) combined with Non-Intrusive Load Monitoring (NILM) neural networks that disaggregate total factory power into machine-specific consumption profiles.
- **Waste Reduction**: Pinpoints idle-state energy waste and peak loads, recovering 10%–20% of avoidable electricity expenses.
- **Automated Catena-X PCF**: Auto-generates Product Carbon Footprint (PCF) data in strict accordance with the **Catena-X PCF Rulebook v4** standard.
- **One-Click OEM Submission**: Direct automated API integration into OEM supplier dataspaces (Cofinity-X / Catena-X connectors), ISO 50001 energy reviews, and CSRD/ESRS E1 statements.
- **BAFA Subsidy Support**: Integrated wizard pre-fills BAFA EEW Module 3 grant paperwork, unlocking 35%–45% state co-funding for our customers.
